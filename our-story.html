Our Story

A home kitchen that never stopped acting like one.

Fifteen years, one instinct: bake it fresh, serve it warm, and treat every order like it's for someone you know — because for a long time, it was.

2010

It started with a home oven and a waitlist.

Binge didn't open with a storefront. It opened with a phone number, a home kitchen in Gurgaon, and a founder, Eddie Singh, who baked cakes for neighbours the way most people bake for family — because someone asked, and because it mattered to get it right. Word moved faster than supply. Orders queued up before the icing had set.

How we grew — in order

From one takeaway counter to a small, deliberate family of outposts.

01

The Takeaway Counter

DLF Phase 1, Qutab Plaza Market — Binge's very first standalone shopfront, still our flagship today.

02

The Cafe

A sit-down space followed on Sohna Road, built for quiche, salads, and long lunches, not just pickup.

03

Bulk & Corporate

As offices around Gurgaon grew, so did our catering arm — hampers, office parties, festive orders.

04

Delhi, at Last

Fifteen years on, Binge crossed the city line to Kailash Colony, Delhi — the same kitchen, a new address.

What hasn't changed

Small batches. Real ingredients. No shortcuts to "fresh."

We still bake in small batches, still finish cakes by hand, and still turn away frozen shortcuts that would make the job easier and the cake worse. Every outpost — the original counter, the cafe, our catering kitchen, and now Delhi — answers to the same standard: would this be good enough to serve in Eddie's own kitchen? If not, it doesn't leave ours.
