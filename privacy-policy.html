Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This policy explains what information Binge Bakery & More ("Binge", "we", "us") collects when you use this website, visit an outpost, or place a reservation or catering order, and how that information is used.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as when you:

  • Submit a reservation or catering enquiry through this website
  • Send us a message through our contact form or email
  • Sign up for updates or offers in-store

This may include your name, phone number, email address, preferred outpost, and any notes you choose to share (such as dietary preferences or occasion details).

How We Use Your Information

  • To confirm and manage reservations and catering orders
  • To respond to enquiries sent through our contact form
  • To share offers or updates, only if you've opted in
  • To improve our menu, service, and outposts based on feedback

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Cookies & Website Data

This website may use basic, privacy-respecting analytics to understand which pages are visited most, so we can keep improving it. No personal identifiers are sold or shared with advertisers.

Data Retention

We retain reservation and enquiry details only as long as needed to fulfil your request and for basic record-keeping, after which they are securely deleted.

Your Choices

You can ask us to update or delete your information at any time by writing to sales@bingebakery.co.in. You can also opt out of promotional messages whenever you receive one.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our outposts and services grow. The date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be sent to sales@bingebakery.co.in, or you can write to us at any of our outposts listed on our Visit Us page.
