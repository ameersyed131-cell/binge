Small detail, worth knowing

Every fruit tart sold at Binge is filled after 11am — we found mornings-only prep meant half the day's tarts went soft by evening, so now the pastry chef fills to order, batch by batch, all day long.

What we're known for

The counter that made Gurgaon fall in love with dessert.

Long before "artisanal" was a word anyone used in Gurgaon, Binge was slicing fruit tarts and pulling brownies out of the oven for whoever walked past the DLF Phase 1 market. That instinct — small batches, honest ingredients, nothing frozen and forgotten — is still how we run every outpost today.
